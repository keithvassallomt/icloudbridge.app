Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 25, 2024

Overview

iCloudBridge is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains our practices regarding data collection and usage.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data.

iCloudBridge operates entirely on your local device and does not collect, store, or transmit any information to our servers or third parties. Specifically:

  • We do not collect any personal information
  • We do not track your usage or behavior
  • We do not use cookies or analytics
  • We do not store any data on external servers
  • We do not share any information with third parties

How iCloudBridge Works

iCloudBridge is a local application that syncs your data between Apple services and your chosen destination (such as NextCloud, Bitwarden, or other services). All data processing happens locally on your device. Your data flows directly from Apple's services to your configured destination without passing through our systems.

Third-Party Services

When you use iCloudBridge, you are connecting to:

  • Apple's services: Subject to Apple's privacy policy
  • Your chosen sync destination: Subject to that service's privacy policy

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these services to understand how they handle your data.

Open Source

iCloudBridge is open source software. You can review our code at any time on GitHub to verify our privacy practices.

Changes to This Policy

If we make any changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Since we don't collect any data, any changes would likely be clarifications or updates to better explain our practices.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can contact us through our GitHub repository.
